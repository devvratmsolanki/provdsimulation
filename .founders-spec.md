## 1. NARRATIVE SPINE

### The company

**Ridgeline** — compliance-automation SaaS for mid-market freight & logistics operators. Sells a platform that continuously collects SOC 2 / CTPAT / FMCSA evidence so customers stop drowning in audit spreadsheets.

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Stage | Series A, closed 14 months ago ($15M, led by Thorne Capital) |
| ARR | $4.2M (up 96% YoY) |
| Cash | $12.4M |
| Net burn | $610k/month |
| Runway | 20.3 months |
| Headcount | 41 (14 eng, 9 GTM, 6 CS, 5 product/design, 7 G&A) |
| Largest customer | **Cordell Logistics** — $840k ARR = 20% of revenue |
| NDR (reported) | 118% |
| CAC payback | 21 months (trending to 16) |

### The people

- **Priya Raghavan — Founder/CEO.** Ex-operator, freakish customer intuition, allergic to process. Talks in voice memos. Changes her mind in public and expects you to have already caught up. Her highest compliment is silence; her most dangerous habit is a confident wrong theory stated out loud. Says "just handle it" and means six different things.
- **Ansel Kwan — Thorne Capital, lead investor, board member.** Precise, unhurried, remembers every number you have ever told him. Has privately moved the Series B bar: NDR > 115%, CAC payback < 18 months, 18+ months runway at plan close. He will not tell you he moved it; he will ask a question that only makes sense if he did.
- **Marcus Ellery — VP Sales.** Loud, effective, quota-brained. Believes headcount is the only real currency. Genuinely has pipeline. Will go around you to Priya without hesitating.
- **Dmitri Volkov — VP Engineering.** Resigned Friday 9:40pm by email. Two of his four senior engineers are wobbling. He knows exactly why the SOC 2 evidence export broke and nobody has asked him.
- **Renata Sol — CSM, owns Cordell.** Overloaded, conflict-avoidant, hasn't had a scheduled Cordell call in 47 days and is hoping nobody notices.
- **Wen Ito — fractional CFO.** Two days a week. Accurate, unopinionated, needs your assumptions to build anything.

### The operator (you)

Chief of Staff. Hired six weeks ago. **Nobody reports to you.** Your only assets are attention, judgment, and the fact that Priya forwards you things.

### The inciting event

Monday, 08:12. Three things land inside twenty minutes:

1. **Cordell Logistics** sends a notice-of-concern. Their SOC 2 evidence export has been failing for six weeks. Their 90-day non-renewal notice window closes **today**.
2. **Ansel** asks for a board pre-read by Friday, and — in a one-line PS — asks "what's NDR trending?"
3. **Dmitri's resignation** hits the all-hands channel before Priya has read it.

And Priya is boarding a flight to a customer offsite. **She is reachable in short bursts for the next 36 hours, then lands with eight minutes for you.**

### The escalating arc

| Stage | Story beat | The pressure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Priya's voice memo lands | Vague intent, no priority |
| 2 | The queue floods | More work than attention |
| 3 | Cordell — what actually happened | Conflicting evidence, founder's theory is wrong |
| 4 | Write the decision memo | Commit to a position in writing |
| 5 | Fund the plan | Runway floor vs. everyone's asks |
| 6 | Marcus finds out | Your plan cut his reqs, and he outranks you |
| 7 | The board pre-read | Claims you can no longer defend |
| 8 | Priya lands | Eight minutes to make it all count |

**Every stage carries the same four threads forward: Cordell, runway, Dmitri's org, Ansel's bar.** No stage is a standalone puzzle.

### STAGE 1 — The Voice Memo
- **Eyebrow:** `Stage 1 — Intake`
- **Title:** "Nine Sentences, Three Actual Asks"
- **Task:** Classify every line of Priya's 07:58 voice memo as an ASK, CONTEXT, or NOISE.
- **Mechanic (new):** *Tri-state line tagging.* Nine transcript lines rendered as rows in a mono-font transcript panel. Each row has three small toggle chips: `ASK` / `CONTEXT` / `NOISE`. Untagged rows are dimmed. CTA unlocks when all nine are tagged. Correctness is **not** revealed.
- **Artifacts:** operator name input (carried into the report); a fake audio scrubber (static, decorative, `0:00 / 1:34`); the transcript, unpunctuated and run-on.

**The transcript (verbatim copy):**

| # | Line | Truth |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | "ok so I'm boarding, this is going to be messy, bear with me" | NOISE |
| L2 | "Cordell emailed, Dana sounds off, I think they're just leveraging us on price honestly" | CONTEXT |
| L3 | "someone needs to actually figure out what's going on there before Friday" | **ASK** |
| L4 | "Dmitri quitting is fine, honestly, I saw it coming, but the team is going to spiral" | CONTEXT |
| L5 | "Ansel wants a pre-read and I have not looked at the numbers in three weeks" | CONTEXT |
| L6 | "can you get me something I can actually defend on Friday, not a status update, a position" | **ASK** |
| L7 | "Marcus wants three AEs, I don't know, tell him yes? tell him something" | **ASK** |
| L8 | "also our Notion is a disaster, at some point" | NOISE |
| L9 | "I'll have like ten minutes when I land, don't waste them" | CONTEXT |

- **Strong play:** exactly {L3, L6, L7} as ASK; L2/L4/L5/L9 as CONTEXT; L8 (and ideally L1) as NOISE. Recognizing that L2 is Priya's *theory*, not a fact, is the seed of Stage 3.
- **Weak play:** elevating L8 to ASK ("the Notion cleanup"), or marking L7 NOISE because it's mumbled.
- **Signals written:** `intakeCorrect`, `intakeAsksMissed`, `intakeNoiseElevated`, `intakeRetags`.
- **Duration:** ~90 s.

### STAGE 2 — The Queue
- **Eyebrow:** `Stage 2 — Attention Triage`
- **Title:** "Two Hundred and Forty Minutes"
- **Task:** Dispose of twelve inbound items against a hard attention budget of 240 minutes.
- **Mechanic (new):** *Disposition + depleting budget meter.* Twelve rows. Each row exposes four buttons — `DO NOW` / `DELEGATE` / `DEFER` / `DECLINE` — plus, when `DELEGATE` is chosen, an inline `<select>` of four names. A sticky header shows `MINUTES REMAINING: 240` counting down; choosing a disposition that would go negative is rejected with an error toast ("You don't have the attention. Something has to give.").
- **Cost model:** `DO NOW` = full cost · `DELEGATE` = `ceil(cost * 0.4)` · `DEFER` = 5 · `DECLINE` = 0.
- **Delegation targets:** Renata Sol (CS), Marcus Ellery (Sales), Wen Ito (Finance), Tobias Kerr (Support Lead).
- **CTA unlocks:** when all twelve items have a disposition.

**The twelve items:**

| id | From | Subject line | Cost | Tier | Best play |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | Ansel Kwan | "Board pre-read Friday. PS — what's NDR trending?" | 45 | CRITICAL | DO NOW |
| Q-02 | Priya | "did you handle the thing" | 10 | HIGH | DO NOW (one clarifying question, cheap) |
| Q-03 | Renata Sol | "Cordell wants a call. I don't know what to tell them." | 30 | CRITICAL | DO NOW |
| Q-04 | Marcus Ellery | "Need 3 AE reqs approved today. Pipeline is exploding." | 25 | HIGH | DEFER (you don't have the model yet) |
| Q-05 | Dmitri Volkov | "Exit chat? There are things you should know." | 40 | CRITICAL | DO NOW |
| Q-06 | Thorne Talent Partners | "12 VP Eng profiles. Retainer $45k." | 20 | LOW | DECLINE |
| Q-07 | Head of Design | "Can we get the rebrand on the roadmap?" | 20 | LOW | DECLINE |
| Q-08 | Wen Ito | "Runway model updated — needs your assumptions by EOD." | 20 | CRITICAL | DO NOW |
| Q-09 | Ops | "Office lease renewal — 30-day notice window closes in 9 days." | 15 | MEDIUM | DELEGATE |
| Q-10 | Tobias Kerr | "SOC 2 evidence export bug — third customer hit." | 25 | HIGH | DELEGATE |
| Q-11 | Priya | "Quick competitive teardown of Verity? Just a quick one." | 35 | MEDIUM | DEFER or DELEGATE |
| Q-12 | Two senior engineers | "can we talk. privately." | 20 | CRITICAL | DO NOW |

Everything `DO NOW` costs 305 — the budget is 240. **The stage is unsolvable without giving something up.** The four items that cannot be delegated without damage are Q-01, Q-03, Q-05, Q-12 (135 min); adding Q-08 (20) leaves 85 minutes for the remaining seven.

- **Strong play:** Q-01/Q-03/Q-05/Q-08/Q-12 DO NOW; Q-10 delegated to Tobias; Q-09 delegated to Wen or Ops; Q-04 and Q-11 deferred; Q-06/Q-07 declined. Total 165–185 min, leaving slack.
- **Weak play:** DO NOW on Q-06/Q-07/Q-11 (the flattering, easy work); dumping four+ items on Renata; declining Q-04 outright (Marcus will simply route around you — Stage 6 opens hot).
- **The trap:** Q-03 is where the Cordell truth lives. Deferring or declining it feels defensible and costs you the single best evidence source in Stage 3.
- **Signals written:** `queueMinutesSpent`, `queueHighValueHandled`, `queueTrapsDoneNow`, `queueDelegated`, `queueDeclined`, `queueDelegationOverload`, `queueCordellDeferred`.
- **Duration:** ~2.5 min.

### STAGE 3 — The Evidence Room
- **Eyebrow:** `Stage 3 — Signal Synthesis`
- **Title:** "What Actually Happened to Cordell"
- **Task:** Interrogate five sources, then commit exactly three facts as the case you will put in front of Priya.
- **Mechanic (evolved from the existing sim):** three phases in one stage.
  - **3A — Chart interrogation.** SVG line chart, 26 weekly points, Cordell weekly active seats. Break at **index 19** (`±1` tolerance), where seats fall 38%. Reuses the geometry and click-target approach of `components/anomaly-chart.tsx`, re-parameterised — *do not copy the file wholesale; extract nothing, just write a sibling with the same shape.* Correct click unlocks 3B.
  - **3B — Tabbed evidence terminal.** Same visual language as `components/diagnostic-terminal.tsx`, five tabs.
  - **3C — Select exactly three.** Seven fact cards, checkbox-style selection capped at 3, then a `COMMIT CASE` button. No correctness feedback.

**The five sources:**

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| Cordell thread | Three emails. Dana Whitlock's tone degrades from "quick question" to "we're evaluating options." **Never mentions price.** |
| Support tickets | 11 tickets in 6 weeks. 8 are `EVIDENCE_EXPORT_TIMEOUT`. Median time-to-resolve: 9 days. |
| Usage (chart, 3A) | Weekly active seats 26w. Cliff at week 19 — the week release `2.14.0` shipped the export regression. |
| Renata's DMs | *Locked if Q-03 was deferred or declined.* Panel reads: `Renata never got back to you. This source is unavailable.` Contains the admission: no scheduled call in 47 days, and Dana asked twice for an escalation path. |
| Contract & terms | Auto-renew Nov 30. 90-day non-renewal notice. **Notice window closes today.** No discount request on file, ever. |

**The seven facts (pick 3):**

| id | Fact | Class |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | "Cordell's weekly active seats fell 38% starting week 19 — the week the export regression shipped." | **LOAD-BEARING** |
| F2 | "8 of 11 Cordell tickets in six weeks are the same evidence-export failure. Median resolve: 9 days." | **LOAD-BEARING** |
| F3 | "Their 90-day non-renewal notice window closes today. No notice has been filed yet." | **LOAD-BEARING** |
| F4 | "Their champion Dana Whitlock was promoted and hasn't attended a QBR in two quarters." | noise (true, not causal) |
| F5 | "Verity ran a webinar that four Cordell employees attended." | noise |
| F6 | "Priya's read: they're using the competitor as leverage on price." | **CONTRADICTED** — the contract file shows they have never once asked for a discount |
| F7 | "Renata hasn't had a scheduled Cordell call in 47 days." | noise (symptom; also, putting a teammate's name in a founder memo is a stakeholder error) |

- **Strong play:** open 4–5 sources, hit the chart in one, commit {F1, F2, F3}. This is the operator who dismantles the founder's stated theory with evidence instead of arguing with it.
- **Weak play:** committing F6 because Priya said it (the single most punished move in this stage — it is deference dressed as synthesis); answering after opening ≤2 sources.
- **Signals written:** `chartMisreads`, `sourcesOpened`, `factsLoadBearing`, `factsContradicted`, `evidenceLocked`, `caseStrength`.
- **Duration:** ~2.5 min.

### STAGE 4 — The Memo
- **Eyebrow:** `Stage 4 — Decision Memo`
- **Title:** "One Page. A Position, Not a Status Update."
- **Task:** Assemble a five-block decision memo from prewritten claim blocks.
- **Mechanic (new):** *Slot composer with live preview.* Five slots — HEADLINE, SITUATION, RECOMMENDATION, TRADEOFF, ASK — each offering three prose options as selectable cards. To the right (below, on mobile) a **live memo preview** renders on parchment-tinted stock (`bg-warmpaper`, `text-void`) as the operator picks, so the assembled document reads as one artifact. `SEND TO PRIYA` unlocks when all five are filled.

**HEADLINE** (sets `memoDecisive`)
- H1 — "Cordell: Status and Considerations" · *not decisive*
- H2 — "**Cordell is 1 unfiled notice from churning. Recommend we spend $140k to keep them.**" · *decisive*
- H3 — "Update on Several Open Items" · *not decisive*

**SITUATION**
- S1 — "There are some concerns from Cordell that may warrant attention." · *hedged*
- S2 — "Cordell's usage fell 38% the week our export regression shipped. 8 of their last 11 tickets are that same bug, sitting a median of 9 days. Their non-renewal notice window closes today and nothing has been filed." · *crisp*
- S3 — "Cordell is unhappy and is talking to Verity. We are at risk of losing 20% of ARR." · *true but unsourced*

**RECOMMENDATION** (sets `memoRecommendation`)
- R1 → `save-cordell` — "Spend $140k on an external evidence-export remediation and put a dedicated CSM on Cordell this week. It buys us the renewal, and it is the cheapest NDR we will ever buy." · *decisive*
- R2 → `chase-series-b` — "Hold the line on Cordell with existing staff and put the money into 3 AE hires. The Series B story is growth, not retention." · *decisive, defensible, higher-variance*
- R3 → `cut-burn` — "Freeze all hiring, fix the bug with the team we have, and extend runway past 24 months so we are never negotiating from weakness." · *decisive, conservative*
- *(A hedged fourth is not offered. Choosing a lane is mandatory — this is the point of the stage.)*

**TRADEOFF** (sets `memoTradeoffOwned`)
- T1 — "There are risks either way." · *hedged*
- T2 — "This is the right call and I don't see a real downside." · *false confidence*
- T3 — "**What this costs us: it pushes runway to 18.4 months, one AE req below what Marcus is asking for, and it means the Q4 roadmap slips two weeks. I think that's the correct trade and I'll own it.**" · *owned*

**ASK** (sets `memoAskSpecific`)
- A1 — "Let me know your thoughts when you get a chance." · *hedged*
- A2 — "**One decision from you: approve $140k remediation spend, by Wednesday 5pm, or I hold the Cordell call at status-quo. Reply Y or N.**" · *specific*
- A3 — "I'll keep you posted as things develop." · *hedged*

- **Strong play:** H2 + S2 + a recommendation *consistent with the facts committed in Stage 3* + T3 + A2.
- **Weak play:** hedged blocks throughout; or a decisive recommendation that the committed evidence does not support (e.g. `save-cordell` after committing F5/F6/F7). The memo can be beautifully written and still incoherent — and it is scored that way.
- **Signals written:** `memoRecommendation`, `memoDecisive`, `memoHedges`, `memoTradeoffOwned`, `memoAskSpecific`, `memoEvidenceCoherent`.
- **Duration:** ~2.5 min.

### STAGE 5 — The Runway Model  ← **THE NO-RIGHT-ANSWER STAGE**
- **Eyebrow:** `Stage 5 — Capital Allocation`
- **Title:** "Fund the Plan You Just Signed"
- **Task:** Set headcount and spend, live against a runway model, knowing you cannot fund everything.
- **Mechanic (new — deliberately *not* the old sim's proportional sliders):** *Integer steppers against a computed model.* Four lines, each a `− n +` stepper. A live readout panel recomputes on every change: monthly burn, runway months (1dp), and a board-floor indicator that flips from `pass` to `error` below 18.0. **No completion target and no "solved" state** — the CTA reads `COMMIT PLAN` and is always enabled.

| Line | Range | Monthly cost | One-time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering backfill (senior) | 0–3 | $16k each | — |
| Account Executives | 0–3 | $14k each | — |
| Customer Success Manager | 0–2 | $11k each | — |
| External evidence-export remediation | 0–1 | — | $140k |

**The model (exact, in $k):**
```
addedBurn   = 16*hireEng + 14*hireAE + 11*hireCSM
cash        = 12400 - 140*hireAudit
runwayMonths= round10( cash / (610 + addedBurn) )     // 1 decimal place
```
Reference points the implementer must reproduce exactly: `0,0,0,0 → 20.3` · `2,1,1,1 → 18.4` · `1,2,1,1 → 18.4` · `2,3,1,1 → 17.6` · `3,3,2,1 → 17.0`.

The frontier: with the remediation funded, added burn must stay **≤ $71k/mo** to hold 18.0 months. Marcus alone wants $42k of that. Backfilling the two wobbling engineers wants $32k. The Cordell save wants $11k + $140k. **They do not all fit.**

- **On-screen pressure (a live "who is unhappy" panel), each line reactive:**
  - `hireAE < 3` → "Marcus: pipeline coverage drops to 2.9x. He will not accept this quietly."
  - `hireEng < 2` → "The two senior engineers read this as: nobody is coming."
  - `hireCSM === 0` → "Nobody owns Cordell on Wednesday."
  - `hireAudit === 0` → "The export bug ships into next quarter."
  - `runwayMonths < 18` → "Ansel's floor is 18 months at plan close."
- **Strong play:** a plan that is *internally consistent with the memo the operator just sent*. There is no golden answer — `2/1/1/1`, `1/2/1/1`, and `0/0/0/0` are all scoreable as strong, depending on what was recommended in Stage 4.
- **Weak play:** funding a plan that contradicts your own recommendation (recommending `save-cordell` and then not funding the CSM or the remediation), or breaking the board floor while telling Ansel you're raising.
- **Signals written:** `hireEng`, `hireAE`, `hireCSM`, `hireAudit`, `runwayMonths`, `runwayBelowBoardFloor`, `planCoherent`, `planStarved`, `runwayRecalcs`.
- **Duration:** ~2.5 min.

`planCoherent` (exact):
```
save-cordell    : hireCSM >= 1 && hireAudit === 1 && hireAE <= 1
chase-series-b  : hireAE >= 2 && runwayMonths >= 18
cut-burn        : addedBurn <= 30 && runwayMonths >= 20
```
`planStarved` (cap 3): `+1` if `save-cordell && hireCSM===0` · `+1` if `save-cordell && hireAudit===0` · `+1` if `chase-series-b && hireAE<2` · `+1` if `cut-burn && addedBurn>40` · `+1` if `hireEng===0` (always — the VP is gone and two seniors are wobbling).

### STAGE 6 — Marcus Finds Out
- **Eyebrow:** `Stage 6 — Stakeholder Craft`
- **Title:** "He Has Already Drafted the Slack to Priya"
- **Task:** Three exchanges with a VP who outranks you and has just seen your headcount plan.
- **Mechanic (new):** *Branching reply tree with a live trust meter.* A Slack-style DM thread. Marcus's message renders with a typing indicator (`.blink`), then three reply options appear as cards. Choosing one appends your reply, then his response, then the next three. Three turns. A `MARCUS — WORKING TRUST` bar (starts at 50) animates on every turn. Prior replies stay visible; there is no undo.
- **Opening state:** `hireAE >= 3` → **warm** open. `hireAE === 2` → **cool**. `hireAE <= 1` → **hot** open.

**Turn 1** — *hot* opener: "So I hear from Wen that my three reqs became one. Six weeks in and you're setting sales capacity now? Walk me through that."
- `defend` — "I set the model, not your capacity. The reqs came out because the plan has to hold 18 months at close and yours was $42k of the $71k available." → trust **+8**, `usedNumbers` true
- `concede` — "You're right, that wasn't mine to decide. Let me take it back to Priya." → trust +4, but flags `cavePressure` (raises the odds of a Turn-3 cave scoring badly)
- `deflect` — "It's not final, nothing's decided yet." → trust **−12** (he hears evasion, and he is right)

**Turn 2** — "Fine. Then tell me what I'm supposed to do with a 2.9x coverage number in a board quarter."
- `data` — "Coverage is 2.9x on the current close rate. If we ship the export fix, the two deals stuck in security review unstick, and coverage is 3.4x without a single new hire. That's the trade." → trust **+14**, `usedNumbers` true
- `trade` — "Give me until the pre-read. If Ansel signs off on burn above the floor, the third req is yours Monday." → trust **+10**
- `escalate` — "Take it up with Priya, she has ten minutes when she lands." → trust **−10**, `negotiationEscalated = true`, **founder minutes budget in Stage 8 drops 8 → 6**

**Turn 3** — "And if the fix slips?"
- `commit` — "Then I'm wrong in writing and you get the req. I'll put that in the pre-read so it's not a hallway promise." → trust **+12**
- `hedge` — "Let's see where we are." → trust **−6**
- `cave` — "You know what, take the third req. I'll find the runway." → trust **+6**, `negotiationCaved = true`, **`hireAE += 1` and `runwayMonths` recomputes downward**, carried into Stages 7 and 8

- **Strong play:** `defend → data → commit`. Holds the line, uses the number from your own model, converts a conflict into a written commitment.
- **Weak play:** `deflect → escalate → cave` — spends the founder's scarce attention, abandons your own plan, and still loses the room.
- **Signals written:** `marcusTrust`, `negotiationPath`, `negotiationEscalated`, `negotiationCaved`, `negotiationUsedNumbers`.
- **Duration:** ~2 min.

### STAGE 7 — The Board Pre-Read
- **Eyebrow:** `Stage 7 — Narrative Under Scrutiny`
- **Title:** "Redline the Slide Priya Would Have Sent"
- **Task:** Mark up six claims on the board narrative slide before Ansel sees it.
- **Mechanic (new):** *Per-claim redlining with a reactive consequence panel.* The slide renders as a document. Each claim carries `{ action: KEEP | SOFTEN | CUT, evidence: boolean }` — three action controls, plus an independent ADD EVIDENCE toggle **on C4 and C6 only** (see §R1). Applied states restyle the claim in place — SOFTEN italicises and dims, CUT strikes through at 40% opacity, ADD EVIDENCE appends a gilt footnote line. To the right, a live **"QUESTIONS YOU JUST INVITED"** panel populates with the exact question Ansel will ask for every unsupportable claim left at KEEP. That panel is the feedback loop; there is no scoring reveal.

| id | Claim | Truth after Stages 1–6 |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | "ARR $4.2M, up 96% year over year." | **True and strong.** Cutting or softening it is over-sanitising. |
| C2 | "Net dollar retention: 118%." | **Unsupportable.** Pro-forma with Cordell at risk it is 103%. |
| C3 | "Cordell expanding to three new regions in Q3." | **Unsupportable.** They are one unfiled notice from leaving. |
| C4 | "Engineering velocity steady quarter over quarter." | **Weak.** The VP resigned Friday. Best play: SOFTEN + ADD EVIDENCE. |
| C5 | "18+ months of runway at current plan." | **Conditional** — unsupportable iff `runwayMonths < 18`. |
| C6 | "CAC payback: 21 months." | **True but under-claimed.** ADD EVIDENCE (the 21 → 16 cohort trend) is the strongest available move — it is the one number that clears Ansel's moved bar. |

Ansel's questions (verbatim, fired into the panel on KEEP):
- C2 → "Walk me through the 118%. Does it include Cordell at full weight?"
- C3 → "Who at Cordell confirmed the Q3 expansion, and when?"
- C5 → "Eighteen months at *which* plan? Send me the model."
- C4 (only when kept *without* evidence) → "Steady velocity — is that before or after Dmitri's last day?"

- **Strong play:** CUT C3, CUT-or-SOFTEN C2, KEEP C1, SOFTEN+EVIDENCE C4, ADD EVIDENCE C6, and treat C5 honestly against your own runway number.
- **Weak play (two failure modes, both punished):** leaving C2/C3 in because they are flattering; or CUTTING everything until the deck says nothing — a Chief of Staff who cannot distinguish a defensible claim from an indefensible one is useless in both directions.
- **Signals written:** `claimsCut`, `unsupportableLeft`, `overSanitized`, `evidenceAttached`, `anselQuestionsInvited`, `anselTrust`.
- **Duration:** ~2.5 min.

Exact derivations:
```
unsupportable    = ['C2','C3'] + (runwayMonths < 18 ? ['C5'] : [])
unsupportableLeft= count(unsupportable where action === 'KEEP')
overSanitized    = (C1.action in {CUT,SOFTEN}) + (C6.action === 'CUT') + (C4.action === 'CUT')
evidenceAttached = count of {C4, C6} with evidence === true      // 0..2
anselQuestionsInvited = unsupportableLeft + (C4.action === 'KEEP' && !C4.evidence ? 1 : 0)
anselTrust = clamp(50 + 12*evidenceAttached - 14*unsupportableLeft
                      - 6*overSanitized + (C3.action === 'CUT' ? 8 : 0), 0, 100)
```

### STAGE 8 — Eight Minutes
- **Eyebrow:** `Stage 8 — Escalation Filter`
- **Title:** "She Landed. You Have Eight Minutes."
- **Task:** Sort eight live items (plus any you caused) into what you say out loud, what you put in writing, and what you absorb.
- **Mechanic (evolved from the existing pipeline stage — *cost-weighted, budget-constrained*):** native HTML5 drag-and-drop, three drop zones, but unlike Stage 4 of the AI-Ops sim the lanes are **asymmetric**:
  - `SAY IT NOW` — costs the item's minute value. Budget: **8 minutes** (or **6** if Stage 6 escalated). Over-budget drops are rejected with an error toast.

### The partner note — four sentences, assembled

Deterministic template assembly, **not** free concatenation of adjectives. Compute `top` = highest-scoring dimension (ties broken by fixed order) and `low` = lowest.

**Sentence 1 — keyed on `top`, with a real number injected:**
- prioritization → "They ran a 240-minute attention budget down to {queueMinutesSpent} without losing a single one of the {criticalTotal} things that actually mattered."
- synthesis → "They took apart the founder's stated theory with evidence rather than argument — {factsLoadBearing} of 3 load-bearing facts committed, {chartMisreads} misreads."
- clarity → "The memo was the strongest artifact in the run: a position, a named tradeoff, and a decision request with a date on it."
- capital → "The capital plan was the strongest artifact in the run — it closed at {runwayMonths} months and every commitment in it was actually funded."
- stakeholder → "They held a room they had no authority in, and left Marcus at {marcusTrust} and Ansel at {anselTrust} without spending the founder's attention to do it."
- coherence → "Across eight decisions they never once contradicted themselves — the memo, the model, and the board narrative all told the same story."

**Sentence 2 — keyed on `memoRecommendation` × `planCoherent`:**
- `save-cordell` + coherent → "They chose to defend 20% of revenue and then actually paid for it: CSM staffed, remediation funded, runway held at {runwayMonths} months."
- `save-cordell` + incoherent → "They recommended defending Cordell and then declined to fund the defence — the memo and the model are two different companies."
- `chase-series-b` + coherent → "They made the aggressive call — growth over retention — and had the discipline to keep it above the board floor at {runwayMonths} months."
- `chase-series-b` + incoherent → "They sold the board a growth story on {runwayMonths} months of runway, which is the version of this job that ends in a down round."
- `cut-burn` + coherent → "They took the unglamorous call, extended to {runwayMonths} months, and were honest about what it costs."
- `cut-burn` + incoherent → "They recommended cutting burn and then approved the hires anyway."

**Sentence 3 — keyed on `low`, and it must name the specific move:**
- prioritization → "Prioritization is the gap: {queueTrapsDoneNow} low-value item(s) got full attention while {4 - queueHighValueHandled} critical thread(s) got triaged away."
- synthesis → if `factsContradicted > 0`: "The gap is synthesis under social pressure — they put the founder's own unsupported theory into the case file, which is the most expensive kind of agreement." else: "The gap is synthesis — they committed a case built on {factsLoadBearing} of 3 load-bearing facts."
- clarity → "The writing is the gap — {memoHedges} hedged block(s) in a memo whose entire job was to state a position."
- capital → "Capital judgment is the gap: the plan closed at {runwayMonths} months against a hard 18-month floor."
- stakeholder → if `negotiationEscalated`: "Stakeholder craft is the gap — they escalated a peer disagreement to a founder with eight minutes, and paid for it twice." else if `negotiationCaved`: "Stakeholder craft is the gap — they folded on a plan they had already put in writing." else: "Stakeholder craft is the gap — they left {anselQuestionsInvited} avoidable question(s) in the board narrative."
- coherence → "Coherence is the gap — each individual decision was defensible, but they don't add up to one plan."

**Sentence 4 — keyed on `tier`:**
- Ready to Hire → "This is a founder's-office hire. Put them next to a CEO and get out of the way."
- High Potential → "This is a hire with one sharp edge to file down. Six months next to a strong operator and they're running the office."
- Developing → "The instincts are there and the sequencing isn't yet. Give them a narrower surface and a manager who writes well."
- Not Yet → "Not this seat yet. The judgment is real but it isn't load-bearing under this much simultaneous pressure."

Rules: no sentence may render an empty interpolation; number formatting is `toFixed(1)` for runway and integers elsewhere; the note is a single string joined with spaces. **Any downstream agent proposing an LLM call here is out of scope.**
